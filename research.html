My research focuses on the interplay between the topology of 3-manifolds and the dynamics of flows that live in them. Most of my work so far has been on (pseudo-)Anosov flows. These are flows with an expanding and a contracting direction. (There is a picture of them on the homepage!)
Some of the questions that drive my research include:

    When does a 3-manifold admit a pseudo-Anosov flow? How many? How are these flows related to each other? When does a foliation admit a transverse pseudo-Anosov flow? Conversely, when does a pseudo-Anosov flow admit a transverse foliation? What kind of geometrical information can one infer from a pseudo-Anosov flow?
